Give Every Video an Owner: Assigning Comments That Actually Get Answered

Nov 5, 2026 · 4 min read · By Talk Back Team

On this page

    Ask a team "who's replying to comments on last week's video?" and you'll often get a pause. Everyone assumed someone else had it. The fix is simple and a little old-fashioned: give every video an owner.

    Why ownership works

    An owner knows exactly which comment sections are theirs. They notice new questions, they remember earlier replies in the thread, and they can build a feel for that video's audience. Nothing falls between the cracks because there are no cracks.

    How to split videos

    There's no single right way. Pick the one that matches your team:

    • By series: one person per show or playlist, so they know the topic well.
    • By age: new uploads to your fastest replier, older videos to someone who checks less often.
    • By load: spread busy videos evenly so nobody is buried.

    Whatever you choose, write it down in the tool, not in a chat thread. Assignments people can see are assignments people follow.

    Reassign without drama

    Holidays, sick days and new projects happen. Reassigning a video should take seconds and shouldn't need a meeting. Moving a few videos to someone else for a week is better than letting comments wait.

    A video with an owner gets answers. A video with "everyone" gets silence.

    Look at it from above

    Once videos have owners, you can filter by person and see the whole picture: who's looking after what, and where the load is uneven. In Talk Back, admins assign one or many videos at a time, reassign whenever needed, and filter the video list by assignee.
