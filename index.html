FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Talk Back?

Talk Back is a team workspace for replying to YouTube comments. Admins assign videos to team members, team members write reply drafts, and an admin reviews and approves every draft before it is posted to YouTube.

Who is Talk Back for?

Teams that share one YouTube channel's comment section, such as brands, creators with staff, agencies and community teams, who want several people to draft replies while an admin keeps the final say on what gets published.

Which platforms does Talk Back support?

YouTube. The current workflow is built around YouTube channels, videos and comments. Other platforms aren't available.

How do I connect a YouTube channel?

An admin connects it from Platform Connections. Once connected, the channel's videos are listed under Content.

Can different people handle different videos?

Yes. Admins assign one or more videos to each team member and can reassign them at any time. Team members only see the videos assigned to them.

Can team members post replies to YouTube?

No. Team members sign in with Talk Back accounts, write drafts and submit them for review. They don't get direct YouTube publishing access.

How are replies reviewed and published?

Submitted drafts appear in the admin's approval queue. The admin can edit, reject with a note, or approve a draft, and then posts the approved reply to YouTube.

Are approved replies posted automatically?

Not today. Publishing is a manual admin step. Automatic posting is planned for a future release.
