How it works

From a New Comment to an Approved Reply

Five steps, two roles, one rule: nothing is posted to YouTube until an admin says so. Scroll through the workflow as your team will use it.

When a draft is rejected

Nothing is lost. It comes back better.

A rejected draft goes back to its author with the admin's note. They fix it and submit again, and it returns to the queue. The loop repeats until the reply is right, and only then can it be posted.
