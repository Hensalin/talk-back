Control & safety

Share the Work. Keep the Control.

Talk Back is built around one safeguard: a person you trust reviews every reply before it's public. Here is how that control works.

Admins hold publishing

Team members can write and submit drafts, but only an admin can post a reply to YouTube.

Every draft is reviewed

Drafts go to one approval queue, where an admin edits, approves or rejects them.

Team accounts

Team members sign in with their own Talk Back accounts, never with the channel's YouTube login.

Admin connects YouTube

The YouTube channel is connected once, by an admin, from Platform Connections.

Notes, not guesswork

Rejected drafts come back with the admin's reason, so mistakes aren't repeated.

A clear trail

Recent activity shows who drafted, approved or rejected what, and when.

Today and next

Posting is manual, on purpose.

Right now an admin posts each approved reply. That keeps a person at the very last step. Automatic posting of approved replies is planned for a later release.
