The workflow

Four Steps, One Clear Owner Each

01 — Assign

Give every video an owner

An admin assigns one or many videos to a team member, and can reassign them at any time. Filter the video list by assignee to see who is looking after what.

  • Assign one or many videos at once
  • Reassign whenever plans change
  • Filter videos by assignee

02 — Draft

Write replies in one place

Team members see only the videos assigned to them, read the comments and write reply drafts. Every draft goes straight to the admin's approval queue.

  • Only assigned videos are visible
  • Comments and drafts side by side
  • Submit with one click

03 — Review

Approve, edit or reject

Every submitted draft lands in the admin's approval queue. The admin can edit it, approve it, or reject it with a note the author can read.

  • One queue for every draft
  • Edit before approving
  • Reject with a note the author sees

04 — Post

Only admins publish

Approved replies wait for an admin, who posts them to YouTube. Team members can't publish. Posting is a manual admin step today; automatic posting is planned for a later release.

  • Nothing reaches YouTube without an admin
  • Team members can never post
  • Automatic posting is on the roadmap
